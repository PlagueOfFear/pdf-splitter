Privacy Policy for PDF Splitter
Last Updated: June 2026
PDF Splitter (the "App") is a utility tool designed to help you add page numbers to PDF files and export them as images.
1. Information Collection and Use: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.
   Files: The App processes your PDF files locally on your device. We do not upload your files to any servers.
   Images: The screenshots/JPG files generated by the App are saved directly to your device's gallery and are not accessible by us.
2. Permissions: The App requires the following permissions to function:
   Storage/Media Access: Used solely to read the PDF file you select and save the resulting JPG images to your "Pictures" folder.
3. Third-Party Services: The App does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.
4. Contact Us: If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store.
